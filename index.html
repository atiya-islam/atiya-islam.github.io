Job Market Paper

Physicians’ Location Choice and Access to Healthcare: The Case of Dobbs

I study how practice location-level regulation changes the geographic sorting of physicians across local markets. Using longitudinal physician practice-location data and variation in state legal environments following Dobbs v. Jackson Women’s Health Organization, I estimate a discrete-choice model of OB/GYN physician location. The model separates the decision to move from the choice of destination and is used to quantify how alternative legal environments reallocate physicians and change access to obstetric and gynecologic care.
