Research

Physicians’ Location Choice and Access to Healthcare: The Case of Dobbs Job Market Paper

I study how practice location-level regulation changes the geographic sorting of physicians across local markets. Using longitudinal physician locations and variation in state legal environments following Dobbs, I estimate a discrete-choice model of OB/GYN location and quantify how alternative legal environments reallocate physicians and change access to care.

Role of Information on Physicians’ Prescription Choice: Evidence from FDA Accelerated Approval

I study how physicians adjust prescribing when the FDA Accelerated Approval program changes information about a drug’s clinical value, focusing on how information frictions shape treatment choice in pharmaceutical markets.

Energy Transition in the Context of the First Market-Based Cap-and-Trade Program in the U.S.
with Emily Pakhtigian

We examine how electric-generating plants adjust operation, generation, and fuel use in response to carbon-pricing incentives under the Regional Greenhouse Gas Initiative.
